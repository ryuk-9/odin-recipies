Pizza

Description

Pizza is a globally beloved dish that originated from simple flatbreads in Naples, Italy. At its core, it features a leavened wheat dough crust baked at high temperatures, traditionally layered with rich tomato sauce and melted mozzarella cheese. Over the centuries, pizza has transformed from humble street food for laborers into a versatile culinary canvas. Today, it spans countless regional and international styles—from the classic Neapolitan Margherita and folded New York slices to thick Chicago deep-dish and crispy Detroit squares—accommodating an endless array of meat, vegetable, and cheese toppings. Whether shared with friends at a local pizzeria or enjoyed as a quick comfort meal, pizza remains one of the world's most popular comfort foods.
