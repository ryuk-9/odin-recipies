Butter Chicken

Description

Butter chicken, or murgh makhani, is a world-famous North Indian curry made from tender, tandoor-roasted chicken simmered in a velvety, spiced tomato and butter-based gravy. Invented in the late 1940s by chef Kundan Lal Gujral at the Moti Mahal restaurant in Daryaganj, Delhi, the dish was originally created as a resourceful way to use leftover tandoori chicken by soaking it in a rich emulsion of butter, cream, and aromatic spices like kasuri methi. Known for its mild, sweet, and comforting flavor profile, it has evolved from a local Delhi specialty into a global culinary icon most often paired with warm garlic naan or basmati rice.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Yogurt
  • Ginger-Garlic Paste
  • Chilli Powder
  • Garam Masala
  • Turmeric

Steps to Make it

  1. Marinate the Chicken
  2. Sear or Grill the Chicken
  3. Build the Makhani Gravy Base
  4. Simmer and Blend the Sauce
  5. Combine and Finish
