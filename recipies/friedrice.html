Fried Rice

Description

Fried rice is a classic, high-heat stir-fry dish made from previously cooked, cooled rice tossed in a wok or large pan. The rice is combined with aromatic ingredients like garlic and onions, alongside scrambled eggs, crisp vegetables, and proteins like chicken or shrimp. Every grain is coated in savory seasonings like soy sauce and toasted sesame oil during the fast-paced cooking process. The result is a smoky, savory dish that perfectly balances fluffy yet distinct grains of rice with tender-crisp additions.

Ingredients

  • Cooked Rice
  • Eggs
  • Onion and Garlic
  • Carrots and Peas
  • Soy Sauce

Steps to Make it

  1. Prep and Chill the Rice
  2. Scramble the Eggs
  3. Sauté Aromatics and Vegetables
  4. Stir-Fry the Rice
  5. Season and Serve
